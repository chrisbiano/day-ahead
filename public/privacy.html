Day Ahead

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Day Ahead brings your calendar, your tasks and your email into one place so you can see your day at a glance. To do that it needs access to some of your Google data. This page explains exactly what it touches, why, and what it will never do.

Day Ahead is operated by Fast Rose Creative, the registered trading name of an incorporated company, which is the data controller for the information described here. Full company details are available on request.

Day Ahead does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use your email or calendar content to train any AI model.

What we access, and why

  • Google Calendar (read-only). To show your events on your daily schedule. Day Ahead never creates, edits or deletes anything in your calendar.
  • Gmail (read and modify). To sort incoming mail into what needs a reply, what's worth reading, and what's noise — and to act on your instruction: mark read, archive, trash, or send a reply you have written. Day Ahead does not request the scope that allows permanent deletion.
  • Your email address and basic profile. To create your account and show which mailbox is which.
  • Contacts you have emailed. Only to suggest addresses when you forward a message.

What we store

  • Your tasks, notes and preferences — created by you, in the app.
  • For each recent email: sender, subject, a short preview, and the sorting decision. Enough to show your inbox summary. Full message bodies are read to sort a message and are not retained.
  • Crash reports and any problem you choose to report, so bugs can be fixed.

Data is stored with Supabase (Postgres) and isolated per account by row-level security, so one account can never read another's. Your Google access tokens are held server-side and are never exposed to the browser.

AI processing

Sorting your mail and writing your daily brief is done by Anthropic's Claude API. Content is sent to that API only to produce a result for you. Anthropic does not train models on data submitted through its API. Day Ahead only ever proposes — sending, archiving, trashing and calendar changes all require you to click.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone. It is processed only by the services that make the product work: Google (your own data), Supabase (storage), Anthropic (AI processing) and Vercel (hosting).

Your control

  • Disconnect any mailbox at any time in Settings — that revokes Day Ahead's access to it.
  • Revoke access entirely at your Google Account permissions.
  • Ask for your account and its data to be deleted, and it will be removed.

Limited Use disclosure

Day Ahead's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
