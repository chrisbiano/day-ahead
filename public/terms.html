What the service does

It acts on your mail only when you tell it to. Sorting is automatic; sending, archiving and deleting are not. Nothing leaves your account without a tap from you.

The daily brief and the inbox sorting are produced by an AI model. Models get things wrong. Read before you send, and check before you act on a summary of something important.
